Southampton Pride 2022

18 November 2022 · By Hannah

It came that time of year again for #southamptonpride. This was my second time attending, and it just keeps getting better and better.

I was so excited to go to this event; I had been looking forward to it for months. I was so excited to see all the amazing people who attend. The guest house I stayed at (Linden Guest House) was lovely, clean and only two minutes from Southampton Central train station. The lady who ran the guest house was friendly and helpful, and I would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for a place to stay in Southampton. The only issue I had was that neither of the front-door keys I was given would work, which wasn't great, but she said she would get it looked at.

This year I hung out with a group of cool people, who invited me to join them for the pride parade. It happened a bit differently compared to last year. It started at the same spot, Queens Park, and went through the High Street, but instead of stopping before the entrance, we took the giant Pride flag into the venue. That was so unexpected but so cool.

One thing I didn't like was that after the parade, when we took the giant flag to a nearby park to fold it away, we had to queue again to get back into the venue. It was a bit of a pain, but it was worth it to hold the giant Pride flag in the venue with all the other cool people.

Then around twelve, I met up with a friend and saw my support group lead worker, which was nice. My friend and I went to a furry summer party (a few weeks before I publicly came out as a furry). I only stayed about an hour before heading back to Southampton Pride. There were some great acts, including drag queens, an Adele tribute and loads more.
